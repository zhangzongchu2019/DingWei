Finding
-------
TestCrossMemberMentionRoutesToTargetSessionAndRepliesToSource opened two
websockets then immediately dispatched a cross-member mention.  A client-side
websocket upgrade completes before HandleSessionWS publishes the session in
Hub.sessionClients: publication happens after the onboarding frame.  The test
therefore sometimes asserted delivery before its target had satisfied the
normal routing precondition.  This repair waits for the concrete, observable
Hub registration condition for both source and target.  It does not extend a
sleep and changes no product code or routing semantics.
